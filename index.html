A place to gather

GreenLeaf began with a simple belief: food should nourish both people and the planet. We cook with seasonal vegetables, local grains, and zero-waste intentions. Our little spot in Wapping Wharf is cozy, welcoming, and built around community.

Every dish tells the story of Bristol's growers, the changing seasons, and the joy of sharing a good meal. No gimmicks — just flavour, honesty, and warmth.

🌿 Student discount 15% · Bring your own cup & save £1

From our kitchen to your table

We're a small team of cooks, growers, and dreamers who believe that plant-based eating can be joyful, not restrictive. Our recipes evolve with the seasons — expect cozy stews in autumn, vibrant salads in summer.

📍 Located at Cargo 2, Wapping Wharf. Just a short walk from the harbour. Come as you are, bring friends, and leave feeling good.
